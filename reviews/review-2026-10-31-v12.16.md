- **Verified WARN:** if Gemini replaced its scroller while the 8 s timer was pending, the timer stayed bound to the
  old node and the new scroller could not schedule one.
- **Verified NOTE:** a route without a scroller kept the listener on the old, detached scroller.
- **Verified WARN, being fixed by this PR itself:** no CI gate covered the three behaviours. The three probes join
  `test:probes` and CI in this PR.
- **False positive:** "far landings through `orbScrollToMessage` don't mark the target". Those routes never marked
  before, and each route keeps its `data-acn-*` contract (DEC-055).
- **Accepted as a documented limitation:** the 8 s at-the-top rule can mark a chat complete before a load slower
  than 8 s lands. A later load above the first turn recomputes it, so the note comes back.

**Round 2**
- An append while the view sat at the top made the 8 s timer give up without re-arming. Fixed: it starts the wait
  over.

**Round 3**
- That restart compared against the last scan's record, which could be stale. Fixed: it compares the live turns.

**Round 4**
- A failed far landing left `data-acn-jump-resolved` claiming a resolution. Fixed: it clears the mark.

**Round 5**
- Also validated the round 1–4 fixes: all present and correct.
- **Verified WARN:** a final short load and a sent message in the same scan grew the count by exactly 10, so the chat

- **Verified WARN:** Summary's and the gallery's direct glides did not supersede a far landing in flight. Fixed:
  `ciSupersedeLanding`.
- **Verified WARN:** a landing the user cancelled by scrolling kept the up-front mark. Fixed: the mark is cleared on
  any outcome except "superseded".
