   every waiting key and no keyless request is pending (`_bmIxAllHeld`, `_ix.wantKeyless`). Round 2 caught that round
   1's `wantFor` kept only the newest key. Gates:

   - **N1g:** passes. It fails on a mutant that never drops a held request.

   toast, and it uses the content box (round 5).

3. **The answer jump's 350 ms retry ignored the user's scroll** (round 1). It now has its own `ixWatchUser`. This one

5. **A flag clicked after the URL moved to another chat filed the old message under the new chat** (rounds 3 and 5).
