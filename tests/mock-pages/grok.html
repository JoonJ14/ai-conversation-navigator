What are the latest developments in quantum computing?

Recent breakthroughs in quantum computing include several notable developments:

1. IBM unveiled its 1,121-qubit Condor processor and introduced a modular architecture for scaling beyond single-chip limitations.

2. Google's Willow chip demonstrated error correction below the threshold needed for practical fault-tolerant quantum computing.

How close are we to quantum supremacy in practical applications?

While Google claimed quantum supremacy in 2019 with its Sycamore processor, practical quantum advantage for real-world applications remains an active area of research.

Key areas nearing practical breakthroughs include drug discovery simulations, financial portfolio optimization, and cryptographic applications.

Most experts estimate we are 5-10 years away from quantum computers solving commercially valuable problems faster than classical supercomputers.
