How do neural networks learn?

Neural networks learn through a process called backpropagation. During training, the network makes predictions, calculates the error (loss) between predictions and actual values, then propagates this error backward through the network to adjust weights and biases using gradient descent.
What is the vanishing gradient problem?

The vanishing gradient problem occurs when gradients become extremely small as they are propagated back through many layers during training. This causes the earlier layers to learn very slowly or stop learning entirely, making it difficult to train deep networks effectively.
